# Mirror Ethical Design & Non-Clinical Principles

Mirror is committed to human-centric, ethical digital wellbeing. The application acts as a clear, reflective mirror—presenting neutral behavioral patterns without judgement, diagnosis, or behavioral manipulation.

---

## 1. Absolute Prohibition of Clinical Diagnoses

Mirror **never** diagnoses, screens for, or comments upon clinical conditions or psychiatric disorders.

### Prohibited Vocabulary
The following words and concepts are strictly forbidden in user-facing explanations, notifications, codebase comments, and documentation:
- ❌ Addiction / Addicted / Compulsive
- ❌ ADHD / Attention Deficit
- ❌ Burnout / Exhaustion
- ❌ Depression / Depressive
- ❌ Anxiety / Panic
- ❌ Disorder / Syndrome / Pathology
- ❌ Therapy / Treatment / Intervention
- ❌ Unhealthy / Toxic / Problematic

---

## 2. Objective, Descriptive Behavioral Framing

Mirror replaces paternalistic, shaming screen-time trackers with purely descriptive behavioral observations.

| Traditional Tracker (Prohibited) | Mirror Ethical Behavioral Copy (Mandatory) |
| :--- | :--- |
| *"You are addicted to social media!"* | *"Active in social apps for 52 continuous minutes."* |
| *"Warning: High distraction! Take a break immediately."* | *"Frequent switching detected across 5 applications in the last 10 minutes."* |
| *"Your late-night screen time is ruining your sleep."* | *"Active computer usage recorded between 01:00 AM and 03:00 AM."* |
| *"You have spent too much time on distractions today."* | *"Top application today: Visual Studio Code (4.2 hours)."* |

---

## 3. Explanatory Transparency

Every detected behavioral pattern includes an audit card detailing:
1. **The Exact Heuristic Trigger**: E.g., *"14 application switches detected across 4 apps in a 10-minute window (threshold: 10 switches)."*
2. **The Observation Window**: Clear start and end timestamps.
3. **Machine Learning Corroboration**: Transparently informs the user whether the on-device neural model concurred with the heuristic rule, including the exact confidence score.
4. **Zero Dark Patterns**: No guilt-inducing visual elements (e.g., dripping red timers, sad faces, or locked screens). The user retains full agency and autonomy over their device.
